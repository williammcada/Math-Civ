## 1. Release purpose

## 2. Requested changes

### 2.1 Field Guide (infographic) — Owner-directed in principle, details Proposed

Owner request (14 Oct 2026): an infographic page reachable from the home screen and from any point in the game, explaining the core resources, the battle system and terrain, because new players "are just going to come in and be confused and click through it randomly, lose the battle, and not be very interested."

Proposed design:

- One "Field Guide" with six tabs, opened from the title screen and from a **Guide** button on every screen (including the battle rail). It opens at the tab that fits the current phase.
  1. **The road to Sekigahara:** one diagram of the whole game (4 chapters → 3 orders each → preparations → battle → ending), with each chapter's choices linked to the part of the battle they affect.
  2. **Your four resources:** Rice, Treasury, Materials, Authority — where each comes from, what spends it, what it buys at the battle.
  3. **Allies and enemies:** Kobayakawa's support meter and its five outcomes, Gifu, Hidetada, the Western council.
  4. **How a battle works:** turn order, two actions per unit, each command, morale and routs, the two ways to win.
  5. **Terrain:** each square type with the game's tile art and its effect.
  6. **Before you march:** a short checklist ranked by impact (rations and ammunition, then Kobayakawa, then equipment and Gifu), in words rather than percentages.
- Every number is read from the game's rules files, so the guide cannot drift from the game.
- Code-drawn diagrams plus existing painted tiles and portraits; no new painted art required.
- Later candidate, not in this draft: a first-battle walkthrough with a few in-game pointers.

### 2.2 Visual redesign, battle first — Owner-directed (14 Oct 2026), details Proposed

Owner playtest: "the whole game, especially the battle mode, needs a UI redesign. It's just too text heavy ... It needs to be more visually obvious what my options are, what units I'm selecting ... I can see my students ... just being lost in a wall of text."

Proposed direction (to be shown as a mockup for approval before building):

- **Board first.** On phone the board fills the width (all 10 columns, no sideways swipe); on iPad it takes most of the screen. Text panels shrink to a slim status strip.
- **Big turn banner** in side colour: "YOUR TURN · Naomasa's Cavalry" (blue) or "ENEMY TURN" (red), plus who acts next.
- **Touch control on a turn-based battle; not real-time (owner, 14 Oct 2026: "We're not doing an RTS here").** Touch the units to command them (owner, 14 Oct 2026: "what if we could touch the units to issue commands, like an RTS?").** Turn-based rules stay; control becomes direct. Tapping your active unit opens a ring of command icons around it (Move, Attack, Volley, Defend, Brace, Rally, End). The board then shows only the valid squares for that command.
- **Tap the unit, tap the square.** The active unit pulses gold. Squares it can move to glow green; enemies it can attack pulse red with a crosshair and the expected losses on them. Tapping green moves; tapping red attacks (with one confirm). No need to pick a command first.
- **Icon command bar** fixed at the bottom of the screen for the special orders (Volley, Charge, Suppress, Defend, Brace, Rally, End turn). Each is an icon with a one-word label; unavailable ones are greyed with the reason shown only on tap.
- **Unit cards as bars and pips:** troop bar, morale bar (green → red), ammunition pips, equipment stars, instead of sentences.
- **Enemy plans as arrows on the board**, not a text list; detail on tap.
- **Campaign screens:** order cards with icons and cost/gain chips (+120 Rice, −80 Treasury) instead of paragraphs; long explanations move behind a "?" and into the Field Guide.
