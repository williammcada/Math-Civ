## 1. Release purpose

v0.3.1 plays correctly but feels flat, and players cannot tell where the story happens or why places matter. The v0.3.1 audit (13 Oct 2026) also found math tasks whose answers do not match what the game charges, choices with no effect, and a battle decided by a few hidden cliffs.

v0.4 makes the story and geography visible (chapter scenes plus an animated campaign map), fixes the math-consequence mismatches, and rebalances the campaign so resources and diplomacy matter in visible, gradual ways.

- **Current verified release:** none. v0.3.1 source identity is verified; functional, device and deployment verification remain Not run (`MIGRATION-BASELINE.md`).

## 3. Requested changes

### 3.1 Chapter story scenes — Owner-directed

**Requirement:** every chapter opens with an animated scene of **at least three story panes**, followed by **an animated map beat** that shows where events happen and why each place matters. Players must learn the locations of Edo, Aizu (Uesugi), Oyama, Fushimi, Osaka, Kiyosu, Gifu, Ōgaki, Mino/Sekigahara and Matsuo hill.

**Scene player (one shared component, data-driven):**

- A scene is an ordered list of beats. Each beat has: image, pan/zoom (start and end crop), duration, one-line caption, an optional speaker portrait with one line, a sound cue, and an optional branch condition.
- Controls: tap/click or Space/Enter advances; **Skip scene** is always visible; **Replay scene** is available from the Chronicle.
- Reduced Motion replaces pans/zooms with cross-fades. Captions stay on screen until advanced, never timed out.
- U-11: scene captions are story only. Task directions ("Choose your council policy") appear after the scene in a separately labelled *Your task* block.
- Works offline inside the single HTML file; no network assets.
- Each scene ends on the map beat (§3.1 table). The map beat hands off to the campaign map (§3.2) in the same view, so the player continues from where the scene left off.

### 3.2 Campaign map redesign — Owner-directed (map movement), details Proposed

- **Base map:** a single illustrated map of central Honshu, Edo to Osaka, with coast, Kiso mountains, Lake Biwa, the Tōkaidō and Nakasendō, and province names. Node positions must be geographically plausible. Add nodes for Matsuo hill (Kobayakawa), plus Ueda only if §3.5's route choice is approved.
- **Labels on an SVG/HTML layer** above the art, so they stay crisp at any scale. Add a zoomed inset for the Mino cluster; v0.3.1 labels for Gifu, Ōgaki and Sekigahara overprint each other.
- **Place buttons must not cover the map.** In v0.3.1 they cover Osaka and Fushimi.
- **Army tokens for both sides:** banner colour, commander portrait, approximate strength ("≈ 3,400"), status. Western tokens: Ishida (Ōgaki), Uesugi (Aizu), Mōri (Osaka), Ukita, Kobayakawa (Matsuo).
- **Animated movement:** tokens travel along roads at each chapter milestone and in the Western council scene. Planned routes are dashed and labelled in days of march, not "travel points".
- **Info card on tap:** who is there, strength, loyalty band in words, and which orders affect this place.
- **Plain-language statuses:** replace the raw values "Player", "Toyotomi Seat" and "Tokugawa Garrison" with sentences.
- *Proposed:* show each order as a pin on the place it affects, and allow issuing orders from the map.

### 3.3 Math consequence and legibility fixes — Proposed

| ID | Change |
| --- | --- |
| M1 | Crisis math must match the cost charged. Select or generate the crisis variant from the rolled severity so the answer equals the amount paid (famine, rebellion, flood, plague, raid). |
| M2 | The adviser shows a worked solution generated from the student's own operands, e.g. "4 + 2 + 2 = 8 parts · 4800 ÷ 8 = 600 · 4 × 600 = 2400 farm workers", with human field labels (no `choiceId`, `district_a`). |
| M3 | The battle-provision adviser shows each formation's division and rounding (3200 ÷ 300 = 10.67 → 11) before adding and multiplying. |
| M4 | Lists of soldiers and rations are printed with commas or a per-formation table, never with ":" (reserved for ratios). |
| M5 | Where the game rounds issued rations for the student (march ÷ 100, battle ÷ 300, rounded up), say so in one sentence, and explain why march and battle rations differ. |
| M6 | Crisis costs that are rounded show the rule ("rounded to the nearest whole") or use base costs divisible by 4. |
| M7 | Muster summary shows the actual soldiers added and the per-formation split. |
| M8 | Audit Stores uses the player's actual chapter ledger (opening, received, spent). |
| M9 | After a wrong answer the question and inputs stay visible; multi-field answers mark which field(s) are wrong without revealing the value. |
| M10 | U-01: the document `<title>` carries the version; the engine version constant matches the build (v0.3.1 still says `0.3.0` internally). |

### 3.4 Battle balance — Proposed

| ID | Change | Audit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| B1 | Kobayakawa: a visible loyalty meter; Ishida bids against you each chapter; replace hard 40/70 thresholds with graded outcomes (joins late, holds, joins with a reduced force). | Win rate 83% committed / 63% uncertain / 0% opposed. Perfect diplomacy reaches 71 against the threshold 70. |
| B2 | Tighten the economy so maximum rations, equipment and ammunition cannot all be bought. Target: the player must give up at least one of the three at full math accuracy. | Bot campaigns could afford 19–36 ration rounds (cap 12), 430–860 Treasury (480 maxes equipment), 300–700 Materials (120 maxes ammunition). |
| B3 | A confirmation warning before departing with no rations or no ammunition, stating the consequence. | No rations 11% wins; no ammunition 3%. |
| B4 | Target battle length about 10–12 rounds: raise morale loss per hit, confirm only attacks (not moves), and match the ration cap to the expected length. | Median 17 rounds; cap 12 + resupply 4 acts as a hidden timer. |
| B5 | After each enemy action, pause with a one-line battle report until the player taps; attacks get a ~1 s lunge/hit/number exchange. | Enemy acts every 0.55 s; result list hidden behind a button. |
| B6 | Western momentum adds Western troops, so the AI's choices change the battle. | West raised troops 3 times in 320 decisions. |
| B7 | Endings: Rally spending and shattered formations in a victory do not count as collapse; a lost Sekigahara has its own ending text instead of "A Narrow Ascendancy". | Seed 7: perfect math, victory, realm 73 → Bad ending. |

### 3.5 Choices with no effect — Proposed (wire or remove each)

- "Advance Rapidly": the +15% field-food penalty never applies because the army becomes active after Chapter 2's harvest.
- "Main Force by Tōkaidō" / "Divide the Columns": the flags `main_force_tokaido` and `hidetada_delay_risk` are never read. *Proposal:* a split route risks Hidetada's late arrival at Sekigahara.
- Intelligence ("Scout information") is never read. *Proposal:* low intelligence shows Western strength on the map as a range or "?".
- Western momentum affects only crisis odds.
- `heavy_rain` and `poor_harvest` are read but never set.

### 3.6 Presentation and content — Proposed

- Unit-type letters on the battle board are readable (currently 7 px inside a 640×480 canvas).
- Ukita has his own portrait (he currently shows Ishida's).
- Compress the four 2 MB WAV music tracks to keep the single file small after new art is added.
- Officers assigned to orders (ROTK-style): moved to §4 for a later version unless approved.

### 3.7 Owner DEV playtest mode — Owner-directed (handbook U-13), added 14 Oct 2026

William asked for a DEV mode to check art, story and gameplay without doing the math (14 Oct 2026). Handbook U-13 (approved 13 Oct 2026) sets the behaviour.

- **Where:** a separate standalone file, `PLAY-SEKIGAHARA-0.4.0-DEV-PLAYTEST.html`, built from the same source with `npm run build:dev` (`__DEV_BUILD__` flag). The classroom file contains no DEV setup panel or banner.
- **No gates:** every order, crisis, provision and war-chest calculation is answered correctly by the same engine the classroom game uses; math dialogs never open. Results and consequences are shown as normal.
- **Starting point:** Chapter 1, 2, 3, 4, battle preparation, or Sekigahara deployment. Earlier chapters are played automatically with fixed sensible choices.
- **"Power-ups" (campaign preparations), any combination:** Gifu secured; Tōkaidō repaired; weather scouts; Hidetada's column; ample stores; fully supplied army; Kobayakawa band (5 choices); fieldworks tier; scout information level. Story scenes on/off, plus a scene gallery.
- **No records:** DEV runs never write save slots or scene-seen records; only the DEV setup choices are remembered on the device.
- **Banner:** "DEV PLAYTEST · The Road to Sekigahara v0.4.0 · no questions, no records" on every screen. "DEV setup" returns to the setup panel.
- **Not changed (needs a decision):** the classroom file's title screen still links "Battle Practice / DEV", which U-13's owner-only boundary would move out of student navigation.

## 4. Out of scope for v0.4

- New nations, leader campaigns, multiplayer, larger battle maps, or an engine rewrite (carried from v0.3).
- Officer-assignment system for orders (candidate for v0.5).
- Voice acting or full-motion animation; scenes are still-image sequences.
- Backend, accounts, telemetry or network assets.

1. **Scenes respect settings:** Reduced Motion → cross-fades; Skip and Replay always available; never block play on audio.
2. **Story/task separation (U-11):** applies to every scene and the screen after it.
3. **Historical honesty:** scenes depict the compressed-history framing already disclosed; the convoy stays labelled fictional.
4. **Art-production path — decided by William McAda, 13 Oct 2026:**
   - **Campaign map: code-drawn.** The code-drawn map sample (real coastline, both sides' tokens, animated movement, Mino zoom, tap-for-info cards) is approved as the direction for §3.2. Claude builds it in code.

   - Handoff packet: [`docs/art-handoff/v0.4/`](../art-handoff/v0.4/README.md). It contains 28 shots with ready-to-paste prompts, a style guide with a style-anchor block, reference images exported from the existing art, character continuity notes, and delivery rules (PNG 3:2 1536×1024 for scenes; 1024×1024 for portraits; Claude crops and converts to 640×360 / 96×96 WebP).
   - Audit addition: in v0.3.1 the Chapter 2, 3 and 4 event plates and all five crisis plates are the same placeholder image with a label stamped on it. The packet replaces all of them.
   - The scene player and map can be built first with placeholders; painted art is integrated as batches arrive in `art-source/v0.4/`.
5. **Deployment:** primary delivery remains the offline HTML file. If any student-facing hosted address is added, U-12 applies (Netlify).

## 9. Release notes (draft, for the implementation checkpoint)

**Implemented:** §3.1 scene player and all storyboard scenes with ChatGPT-painted art (28 shots); §3.2 geographic map (both sides, statuses, order pins, days of march, Mino close-up); M1–M10; B1–B7; §3.5 (Advance Rapidly, Hidetada, Tōkaidō initiative, heavy rain, poor harvest, scout information, Western momentum troops); §3.6 (readable unit letters, Ukita portrait, MP3 music: 13.3 MB → 6.9 MB).

**Balance values chosen in implementation:** morale per hit 9 + 0.014 per basis point; 800 Western levies; Gifu garrison 600 joins the West if Gifu is not taken; Kobayakawa's entry costs the other side 10 morale; equipment 120 Treasury per point; ammunition 20 Materials per charge; scout information 60 = exact, 35 = estimates.

**Also fixed:** 18 strings missing from the recovered v0.3.1 source (ending clauses, convoy names).

**Unresolved:** Rice and Materials do not constrain battle purchases; weak setups still produce 17–20 round battles; the map tab does not animate movement between visits.

**Manual checks still needed:** William's play-through and art approval in context; physical iPad (landscape), iPhone and Windows; audio on devices; export/import in a browser.

**Delivered:** `release/PLAY-SEKIGAHARA-0.4.0.html` and the owner file `release/PLAY-SEKIGAHARA-0.4.0-DEV-PLAYTEST.html` (built from the checkpoint; not committed — `release/` is ignored). Canonical repository updated: yes (`main`).
